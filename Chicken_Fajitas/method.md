Instructions
In a bowl, combine the chicken with 1 tbsp olive oil, paprika, cumin, chilli powder, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
Heat a large frying pan over medium-high heat.
Cook the chicken for 5-7 minutes until browned and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
Add the remaining oil to the pan and cook the peppers and onions for 4-5 minutes until slightly softened but still crisp.
Return the chicken to the pan and stir everything together for 1-2 minutes.
Warm the tortillas in a dry pan or microwave.
Serve the chicken and vegetables with the tortillas and your chosen toppings.
