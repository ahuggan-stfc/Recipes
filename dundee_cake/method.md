<ol>

<li>Preheat the oven to 150C/140C Fan/Gas 2. Grease and double-line a 20cm/8in loose-based deep cake tin with greaseproof paper.</li>

<li>Beat the butter and soft light brown sugar in a food processor for 3–4 minutes, or until very light and fluffy.</li>

<li>Add the marmalade and mix for a few seconds. Slowly add the eggs, one at a time, beating well after each addition.</li>

<li>Add the flour, almonds and spices to the batter. Mix slowly until well combined, then stir in the mixed dried fruit and cherries with a large metal spoon. Add the whisky or milk and mix until well combined.</li>

<li>Spoon the mixture into the cake tin, smooth the surface and carefully arrange the blanched almonds in circles on top.</li>

</ol>
